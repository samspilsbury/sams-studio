How it plays

Save the world. Take the credit.

Everyone picks a country (the eldest player gets Djibouti, no arguments). Then, turn by turn, disaster comes to you.

1

Roll for trouble

Roll the die and draw that many Crisis cards. Read them out loud. Peruvian turnip food poisoning? That's your problem now.

2

Throw aid at it

Play Foreign Aid cards that match the crisis category (health, environment, politics or science) and beat its value. A mug of breakfast tea sorts out food poisoning nicely. Every crisis you fix is points in the bag.

3

Cut a deal

Can't fix it alone? Ask around. Anyone who helps you earns a Power card, so there's always someone keen to "help"… for a price. Haggle hard. It's your turn, so you get the final say.

4

Chip in for the planet

Donate an aid card to one of four Global Crises: global warming, pandemic, market collapse or rogue AI. They're the game clock. Solve all four and it's game over.

Leave a mess behind and the next leader inherits it. Let the crises on the table pile up to 15 points and whoever rolled last goes into national lockdown, losing their best crisis card.

How to win: finish with the most points in any single category.
