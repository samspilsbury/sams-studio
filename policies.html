Returns & refunds

You can cancel your order and return the game within 14 days of receiving it for a full refund, under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations (EU customers have the same 14-day right under EU consumer law). The game must be unused and in its original condition. Email us first to arrange the return; any return postage is paid by you unless the item is faulty.

If your game arrives damaged or with missing cards, contact us within 30 days and we'll replace the missing parts or refund you in full, including postage.

Refunds are made to the original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the return.
